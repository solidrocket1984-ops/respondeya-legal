## English

**Capa Privada — Privacy Policy (short version for extension stores)**

Capa Privada detects and masks personal data on your own device before you send text to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Copilot. The text you process never leaves your device: RespondeYA does not receive it, store it, or have access to it.

We only process the data needed for your account and license: email, subscribed plan, seats (if applicable), and payment data, handled by Stripe. We do not collect any usage data from the extension, no browsing analytics, and no log of what we mask.

The extension only contacts our servers to verify your license and to download the detection model; never to send the text you process.
